Rich Punjabi Culture

Punjab is known for its vibrant traditions, energetic music, colorful celebrations, and warm hospitality. Bhangra and Giddha are two of the region's most famous traditional dances.

Famous Punjabi Food

Punjabi cuisine is loved around the world for its rich flavors, generous use of spices, and hearty meals.

  • Amritsari Kulcha
  • Sarson da Saag and Makki di Roti
  • Chole Bhature
  • Butter Chicken
  • Lassi

Places Worth Visiting

Punjab offers historic landmarks, beautiful religious sites, lively cities, and peaceful countryside.

Three places to explore

  1. Golden Temple, Amritsar
  2. Wagah Border
  3. Jallianwala Bagh, Amritsar
